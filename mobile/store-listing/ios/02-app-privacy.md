# MAINTAINR — App Store Connect "App Privacy" answers

Fill this in **App Store Connect → your app → App Privacy**. This is the
"nutrition label" Apple shows on the product page. Answer honestly against what
MAINTAINR actually collects (see the live Privacy Policy at
`https://maintainr.plainspokenfoundrynine.com/privacy`).

---

## Step 1 — "Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?"
**YES.**

(MAINTAINR collects an account email, the maintenance data users create, and
diagnostic/usage data. So the answer is Yes.)

---

## Step 2 — Data types collected

Add each of the following. For every type, the global answers are:
- **Used for tracking?** → **NO** (we do not track users across other companies'
  apps/sites, and we do not share data with data brokers or ad networks.)
- **Linked to the user's identity?** → **YES** for everything tied to the account
  (email, content). Diagnostics are **NOT linked** (see notes).

### Contact Info → Email Address
- Collected: **Yes**
- Purposes: **App Functionality** (account sign-in, org separation)
- Linked to identity: **Yes**
- Used for tracking: **No**

### Identifiers → User ID
- Collected: **Yes** (org/user account identifier)
- Purposes: **App Functionality**
- Linked to identity: **Yes**
- Used for tracking: **No**

### User Content → Other User Content
- Collected: **Yes** — the assets, work orders, maintenance schedules, parts,
  technicians, downtime events, and related records users create.
- Purposes: **App Functionality**
- Linked to identity: **Yes**
- Used for tracking: **No**

### Usage Data → Product Interaction
- Collected: **Yes** — basic interaction/analytics (e.g. sign-in events) to
  operate and improve the service.
- Purposes: **Analytics**, **App Functionality**
- Linked to identity: **Yes** (interaction is tied to the account)
- Used for tracking: **No**

### Diagnostics → Crash Data
- Collected: **Yes**
- Purposes: **App Functionality** (diagnose problems)
- Linked to identity: **No**
- Used for tracking: **No**

### Diagnostics → Performance Data
- Collected: **Yes**
- Purposes: **App Functionality**
- Linked to identity: **No**
- Used for tracking: **No**

## Notes / rationale
- **No camera / no Photos or Videos.** MAINTAINR does not capture photos or use
  the device camera — do NOT add the "Photos or Videos" type (this differs from
  EXTRACTR/LANDLORDR, which do).
- **No location, no contacts, no health, no financial account data, no browsing
  history** is collected — do NOT add those types.
- **No third-party advertising**; we do not use data for tracking. Every
  "tracking" toggle stays **Off**.
- **Sensor readings** in the product are equipment telemetry your organization
  enters/imports — not personal data from the user's device — so they fall under
  "Other User Content," not any device-sensor privacy type.
- **Privacy Policy URL** (required on this screen):
  `https://maintainr.plainspokenfoundrynine.com/privacy`
