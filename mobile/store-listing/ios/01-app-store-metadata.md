## Description  (max 4000 characters)
```
Maintainr is a computerized maintenance management system (CMMS) that helps your team keep equipment running. Track every asset, schedule preventive maintenance, run work orders from request to close-out, and get ahead of failures before they cause downtime.

Built for maintenance teams, facilities, and operations, Maintainr keeps your assets, schedules, parts, and technicians in one place — on your phone or at your desk.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Track your assets
Keep a complete register of equipment across every facility, organized by category, with the full maintenance history for each one.

Work orders
Create, assign, and close work orders. See what's open, what's overdue, and who's on it — so nothing falls through the cracks.

Preventive maintenance
Schedule recurring maintenance so routine service happens on time instead of after something breaks.

Predictive alerts
Sensor readings and predictive alerts flag equipment that's trending toward failure, so you can act before it goes down.

Parts & inventory
Track parts usage against work orders and keep an eye on what's on hand.

Downtime & accountability
Log downtime events and keep an audit trail of the work your team does.

Organized & private
Each organization's assets, work orders, and records are isolated from every other customer on the platform.

This app connects to your existing Maintainr account at maintainr.plainspokenfoundrynine.com. You will need an account to sign in, and a live internet connection is required. See our Privacy Policy for how your data is handled.

Stop firefighting. Predict failures, plan the work, and maximize uptime — with Maintainr.
```
